---

When you have a many-to-many relationship with extra columns on the join table (like a `timestamp`, `status`, or `quantity`), the standard `@ManyToMany` annotation is no longer sufficient. This is because `@ManyToMany` assumes the join table is a "hidden" entity managed entirely by JPA, containing only the two foreign keys.

To handle additional fields, you must promote the join table to a **full JPA Entity**.

You cannot put these extra columns in Class A or Class B because they don't belong to the individual objects; they belong to the **relationship** between them.

Instead of one `@ManyToMany` relationship, you split it into two `@OneToMany` relationships pointing to a new "Link" entity.
- **Class A** has a `@OneToMany` relationship with the **Link Entity**.
- **Class B** has a `@OneToMany` relationship with the **Link Entity**.
- **Link Entity** has `@ManyToOne` relationships with both A and B, plus your **extra columns**.
