- `Eager loading`: related objects are immediately loadedk, this is the default strategy for:
	- `@OneToOne`
	- `@ManyToOne`
- `Lazy Loading`: related objects are loaded when accessed, this is the fefault strategy for:
	- `@OneToMany`
	- `@ManyToMany`

> [!NOTE]
> Any thing ends with `One` it will use eager loading by default.

OneToOne(fetch = FetchType.LAZY)
